Help & Contact

HOW CAN WE HELP?

Answers to common questions, and a direct line to our team.

Which platforms are your games on?

Each title lists its launch platforms on its card. Most of our games support PC and current consoles, and some are on mobile or Nintendo Switch.

How do I follow a game’s development?

Follow our socials from the footer. We post devlogs, playtest invites and release dates there first.

Do you accept fan art or fan mail?

Yes. Choose “Fan Mail” in the contact form and tell us what you made. We read every message.

How can I apply for a job at HypernerdGames?

Choose “Careers” in the contact form and share your portfolio or LinkedIn profile.

Can press get review keys?

Yes. Send a request through the “Press Inquiry” option with your outlet name and audience size.
